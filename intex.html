Rajya Stariya Bal Vaigyanik Pradarshani (RSBVP) is a state-level children’s science exhibition. It encourages students to explore real-life challenges, apply scientific and mathematical thinking, and present creative models and solutions. We are students of Class VIII-A, working together to turn classroom learning into a practical investigation. Through FLOWGUARD, we aim to demonstrate how mathematics and computational thinking can help examine fair and responsible use of an essential resource: water.

01 · The challenge

One limited supply. Different household needs.

Equal division may not reflect different requirements. FLOWGUARD investigates an alternative: protect essential needs when possible, then distribute remaining water according to unmet demand.

💧

Limited supply

The total available water may be lower than the combined demand.

🏠

Different requirements

Each model household has an essential requirement and a maximum total requirement.

⚖️

Fairness is a choice

Different allocation rules define fairness differently. We compare outcomes instead of assuming one method is always best.

02 · Mathematical model

How FLOWGUARD allocates water

For each household i, define essential need mᵢ, total need dᵢ and allocation xᵢ. Let S be the available supply.

Case A · Enough for essentials

S ≥ M

Give each household its essential need, then share the remaining supply in proportion to unmet total demand.

xᵢ = mᵢ + (S − M)(dᵢ − mᵢ)/(D − M)
Case B · Essential shortage

S < M

Share the limited supply in proportion to essential requirements, so each household receives the same fraction of its essential need.

xᵢ = S × mᵢ / M
Case C · Surplus

S ≥ D

Meet each household's total stated demand. Any remaining supply is reported as surplus, not assigned unnecessarily.

xᵢ = dᵢ; surplus = S − D

M = sum of essential needs; D = sum of total needs. The model assumes known demands, divisible water and accurate measurements.

Our project identity

FLOWGUARD at a glance

🎯

Our objective

Explore a transparent way to allocate limited water among households with different essential and total requirements.

🧮

Our approach

Use mathematical modelling, logical conditions and a simulator to compare allocation strategies.

🔬

Our next step

Validate the calculations, test a low-cost physical model and record observations before making real-world claims.

This website currently presents a proposed mathematical model and simulated calculations. It does not claim that FLOWGUARD is already a proven real-world water-distribution system or a nationally selected invention.
